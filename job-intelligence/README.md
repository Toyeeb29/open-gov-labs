# Job intelligence

Maps generalized job responsibilities to labs and competencies.

Pipeline:

```
public job posting (research input)
        ↓
role
        ↓
responsibilities / skills / frameworks / tools
        ↓
recurring tasks
        ↓
lab scenarios
        ↓
competencies
```

## What you may store

- Source URL
- Retrieval date
- Role title (generic)
- Generalized responsibilities written in our own words
- Skills, frameworks, tools
- Lab ids this maps to
- A hash of the source, if needed for provenance

## What you must not store

- Full copyrighted job descriptions
- Large verbatim excerpts
- Proprietary training materials

Research notes that include copied posting text stay on the contributor's machine. They do not get committed.
