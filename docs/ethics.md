# Ethics and legal

- Create original fictional companies. Do not reproduce a real company's internal program.
- Do not copy proprietary training, paid course labs, or vendor certification items.
- Public job postings are research inputs only. Store generalized responsibilities. Never commit full descriptions.
- Cite frameworks via publisher sources. Do not dump copyrighted control catalogs into the repo.
- Simulated personal data must be obviously fake.
- Label portfolio exports as **Simulated Enterprise Project**. Do not encourage users to present labs as employment.
- AI output in-product (when added) must be labeled, reviewable, and unable to approve risk treatment or access.
- GRC engineering labs must not include exploit PoCs, malware, or instructions for unauthorized access.
