# AI Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT — not approved)

Owner: CISO  
Status: Draft since June 2026  
Last editor: Priya Shah

Intended rules (not in force):

- All AI systems in an inventory before production
- High-impact HR, credit, or biometric uses require committee review
- Vendors that train on customer/applicant data need a written opt-out or a prohibition
- Automated adverse decisions require human review before the decision takes effect

There is no approved AI policy as of 20 September 2026.
