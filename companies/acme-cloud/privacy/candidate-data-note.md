# Candidate data — current practice

Privacy Counsel note, 4 March 2026 (still current):

Acme Cloud collects applicant name, email, work history, and CV for roles in the US, Ireland, and Germany. Retention is "until the role is filled plus 12 months" in the careers privacy notice. No automated rejection is described in the notice.

If a vendor ranks or auto-rejects candidates, Privacy must see the flow before go-live. Elena Voss.
