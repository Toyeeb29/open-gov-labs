# Risk rating (internal, one page)

Used when GRC updates the register. Not a quantitative model.

| Rating | Likelihood | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Expected this quarter **and** customer data, production integrity, or legal duty is in play | Immediate harm to customers, reportable event, or failed Type II |
| High | More likely than not this year, or already occurring | Material customer, audit, or regulatory impact |
| Medium | Possible this year | Contained operational impact; compensating control exists |
| Low | Unlikely | Limited, reversible impact |

**Inherent** = rating before treatment.  
**Residual** = rating after current controls, using evidence you can show — not the control description.

Treatment options: mitigate, accept (named executive), transfer, avoid.  
Acceptance of High or Critical requires Alex Kim (CISO) in writing. GRC does not accept those risks alone.
